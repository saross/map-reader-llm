K=5 screen put gemini-3.7-flash ABOVE the whole Gemini 3 plateau on
the gold-standard (GS) corpus (verified best 0.9139 @20 m vs anchor
0.8961; +0.0178, p = 0.17 — under the GS instrument's MDE80 ≈ 0.065

0.013 (MDE80, `results/sensitivity-mde-2026-08-28/`); the observed

(`planning/gemini37-screen-2026-08-28.md` § Escalation, PI-approved
2026-08-29): the resolving test, and the deployment cell the paper
would cite if the family gain is real.

### 2026-08-30 — Flex-to-standard bailout rule (PI, standing)

and flex is bottlenecking (503 storms, retry-exhausted straggler

retries. Established after run_2's 95-tile tail burned 40+ minutes
of flex retries and then cleared in under a minute at standard for
$0.04. Applies to recoveries, pass tails, and small verifier stages;
the tier is stamped in metas either way.
