## ⚠ Caveats — this is a worklist for human adjudication, not a verdict on the science

- **The science is sound; the eval *record* is non-uniform.** Most flags are standardisation gaps (add MCC, full buffers, `n_detections`), not wrong results.
- **`partial-buffers` is corpus-dependent.** The classifier's 'full buffers' = the 4-map standard 20/30/40/50 m. The **55-map** runs legitimately use a larger buffer set (50/75/100/125/150 m) appropriate to that corpus — so `partial-buffers` on the `55maps-*` runs needs human review, not automatic re-scoring at 20-50 m.
- **`stale` is the strongest signal** (eval `n_detections` ≠ current geojson feature count): the eval predates a geojson/GT refresh (e.g. the 2026-05-03 55-map GT update). Re-score to refresh.
- **`no_standard_scoring`** does not mean unscored in every case — e47 / consensus-384-t1-0 / proposer-verifier-384 were scored via the leaderboard / consensus-sweep pipelines; **proposer-verifier-512** and **pv-diag-256** may be genuinely unscored (decide: re-score or passes-only).
