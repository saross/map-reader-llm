## 1. The question

The final 55-map board (2026-08-27) is led entirely by the new
text-modality geometry family. Before the paper claims "text is
stronger for mound localisation under the leading configuration", the
image variant of that configuration must be measured — and image has
historically carried the strongest tile-level Matthews Correlation
Coefficient (MCC), so how MCC holds up is the second question. A
third, emergent question rides along: every prior image cell ran HIGH
thinking; this is the first image run at MINIMAL, so it also probes
the thinking axis for image (PI: trial MINIMAL-vs-HIGH comparisons on
strong configs may follow).

## 3. Measurement contract

Scored with the committed GS stride/grid machinery on the common
footprint against the GS reference; full multi-buffer curve, F1-led
with tile-MCC alongside; K = 10 union → carry-forward verifier →
(prob_t × k) sweep and free first-N ladders — the identical chain to
the text cells, so every comparison is like-for-like.

**Registered expectations (committed at launch; assessed bet-by-bet):**

| # | Bet | Prediction | Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| IP1 | Localisation | text-B > image-B at best-F1 ≤ 30 m, N = 10 | the study's image localisation penalty (image working precision ~75 m vs text ~30 m, S111) |
| IP2 | The gap narrows with buffer | the text−image ΔF1 shrinks monotonically from 20 m to 75 m | same mechanism |
| IP3 | MCC | image-B tile-MCC within ±0.02 of text-B (image's historical strength survives the geometry change) | IM MCC crowns; 55-map board MCC 0.71 class |
| IP4 | Verifier dial | image-B best prob_t ∈ {0.15, 0.20} | the P8 pattern — the dial has transferred everywhere |
| IP5 | Saturation | image saturates SLOWER: N = 3 not within noise of N = 10 | weakly grounded (first MINIMAL image cell); registered as informative either way |

Pre-named informative failures: image ≥ text at ≤ 30 m (the paper
claim would need rewriting); MCC collapse (> 0.05 below text);
prob_t off-lattice.
