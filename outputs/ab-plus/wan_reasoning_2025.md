## Summary (Claude's synthesis — advisory, unverified)

Wan, Wu, Chen and Li's NAACL 2025 long paper attacks the question our consensus-over-passes architecture settles by fiat: how many samples are enough. Self-consistency (Wang et al. 2023) samples multiple chain-of-thought reasoning paths and marginalises over them by majority vote, but the authors' complaint is that it offers no systematic rule for the optimal sample count, so practitioners over-sample; their benchmark configuration is 40 paths per question. Reasoning-Aware Self-Consistency (RASC) replaces the fixed budget with an adaptive stop. Each sampled reasoning-answer pair is scored by a logistic regression over deliberately lightweight features: three answer-level (Local-Consistency, Global-Consistency, Parsing-Error) and seven reasoning-level (path length, step count, an error-admitting flag and four Jaccard-overlap relevance measures). Pairs scoring at or above threshold T enter a buffer; sampling halts when the buffer reaches capacity N. Throughout, N = 5 and T = 0.5, fixed on a training set and carried to test. Aggregation is then weighted majority voting by sufficiency score rather than self-consistency's equal-weight vote, and the highest-scoring rationale is returned as the explanation.

The empirical claim is about cost, not accuracy. Across a main evaluation of 16,725 samples spanning mathematical, commonsense and symbolic question answering with GPT-4, GPT-3.5 Turbo, Vicuna-13B and Llama2-7B, RASC cuts sampling by 60–80 per cent (the abstract's headline is “approximately 70%”) while the significance tables report no statistically significant accuracy difference against self-consistency on any reasoning type. On GPT-4 mathematics, 40 samples at 87.5 per cent become 4.59 samples at 87.5 per cent; averaged per question over all data rather than mathematics alone, wall-clock with GPT-4 falls from 398.9 s to 47.2 s, of which 2.05 s is RASC's feature-extraction overhead. The efficiency claim is conditional, holding only while that overhead stays smaller than the inference saving. That conditional matters to us: RASC's bookkeeping is a regression over string statistics, whereas our adversarial verifier is a full extra model call, so the arithmetic does not transfer for free.

Three further findings bear on live design questions. First, the operating point carries: N and T fixed on the training distribution hold on 10,824 BigBench and StrategyQA examples the paper treats as out-of-distribution, at 14.5 per cent of self-consistency's samples with GPT-3.5 Turbo — no visible transfer penalty, and the shape of result our carried-versus-oracle comparison tests. Second, sample demand scales with difficulty (3.12 samples for Elementary Mathematics, 6.15 for College Mathematics, 8.54 for Abstract Algebra on MMLU with GPT-4), which argues for difficulty-conditional budgets rather than one K per tile. Third, the reasoning-quality features that give the method its name are weak predictors on their own: only the two answer-consistency features exceed |r| = 0.09 against correctness, and the ablation makes reasoning-level features alone the cheapest and least accurate configuration. The gain comes from combining them, and the superiority claim is scoped to the accuracy-versus-sample trade-off, not to accuracy. The demonstration is entirely single-answer text question answering, and the limitations section warns that these hand-crafted features “may not capture all aspects of high-quality reasoning across different domains”.

## Positioning annotation (interpretive)

A consensus / test-time-compute antecedent of the most direct kind: it names the gap our fixed-K consensus sits in — self-consistency has no principled rule for the number of passes — and shows that an adaptive stopping rule recovers most of the cost at statistically indistinguishable accuracy. Its two mechanisms map onto live choices in our architecture: score-weighted majority voting is the alternative to our equal-weight greedy consensus, and its difficulty-conditional sample demand argues against a single K across a ladder that runs from area segmentation to point symbols. It also supplies the cautionary half, which we should carry with the citation: the reasoning-quality signal the method is named for is individually weak, the efficiency claim is explicitly conditional on the scoring overhead staying below the inference saving, and the entire demonstration is single-answer text question answering — no image, no multi-object output, and so no direct analogue of our set-valued per-tile consensus.

### KP5
- **Quote (verbatim):** "they treat all samples equally for sampling decisions, basing the stopping criteria solely on the consistency of final answers, with this uniform treatment extending to the use of majority voting"

- **Paraphrase:** The authors' diagnosis of existing early-stopping self-consistency variants is that they weight every sample identically, both when deciding to stop and when voting; RASC's answer is a sufficiency score used as a vote weight, so that higher-quality reasoning-answer pairs have greater influence on the final decision.
