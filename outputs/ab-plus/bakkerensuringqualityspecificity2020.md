## Summary (Claude's synthesis — advisory, unverified)

Bakker and colleagues ask not whether preregistration helps but whether the form researchers fill in changes how much interpretive latitude survives it. They coded 29 of the 34 "researcher degrees of freedom" catalogued by Wicherts and colleagues, scoring each 0-3 (0 = not restricted; 2 = "specific" and "precise"; 3 = additionally "exhaustive", i.e. the registration states explicitly that no deviation will occur) and averaged them into a Transparency Score. The structured Prereg Challenge format (26 questions plus a Center for Open Science completeness review) beat the unstructured Standard Pre-Data Collection format: median 0.81 against 0.57, U = 2,053, p < 0.001, Cliff's Delta = 0.49 — "large" on the thresholds they adopt, though barely (their cut is 0.474). Two qualifications travel with that headline. Both medians sit near the floor of the 0-3 scale, and the median per-item effect across the 29 degrees of freedom is 0.17, which is "small"; only missing data (A1) and inference criteria (A15) are large in the expected direction. Twenty-two of 29 items favoured the structured format directionally, 14 at p < 0.05, and 5 after a Holm correction the authors did not preregister. Quoting Cliff's Delta = 0.49 without the item-level distribution is precisely the aggregate-versus-component trap.

Three findings bear on our own apparatus. The structured format's advantage concentrates where the form carries an explicit prompt; the items with none — statistical assumptions (A3), analysis software and packages (A14), handling of data during collection (C3) — score near zero in both formats. For a computational study A14 is the analogue of model version, decoding settings, library versions, and seeds: the degree of freedom our setting most needs closed is the one preregistrations most reliably leave open. Second, coders agreed on how many hypotheses a registration contained in only 14.29% of cases (15.09% unstructured, 13.46% structured) — a chastening figure for a paper that registers H1-H15 as though the count were settled. Third, they scored the plan alone, not its correspondence with the final report, and name that correspondence as a separate site of exploitable latitude. Their instrument measures a registration's specificity, not its execution's fidelity — which is the cell our errata log, recorded deviations, and analyses register occupy.

Scope guards before borrowing any of it. Assignment was not random: researchers self-selected the format, and structured registrations had additionally passed a completeness review, so format and review are confounded; coders could not be blinded, because the formats look different. The structured pool was small — 122 public registrations existed, 53 were drawn, 52 survived a withdrawal — against 53 unstructured. 88.6% of registrations came from psychology, and the authors say it will be worth reviewing and updating the coding format for other disciplines to evaluate whether the findings generalise. They decline the cost question outright: whether detailed preregistration repays its preparation effort is not answerable from their data, though they report their own efficiency gains anecdotally, and a pretested average of two hours to code each registration in their own study.

## Positioning annotation (interpretive)

The empirical benchmark against which this project's preregistration apparatus can be scored, and the source of its sharpest self-directed warning; its primary cluster is preregistration and open-science methodology, with methods/metrics hygiene as a secondary. Bakker et al. find that structure helps — a large aggregate effect (Cliff's Delta = 0.49) for a 26-question guided format over an open one — but that the help is thinnest exactly where a computational study needs it, on the degrees of freedom neither format prompts for (statistical assumptions, analysis software and packages, data handling during collection), and that coders could not agree on how many hypotheses a registration even contained (14.29%). Because they deliberately score the plan and not the plan-against-report correspondence, they leave open — rather than occupy — the deviation-checking cell that our errata log, recorded deviations, and analyses register are built for.
