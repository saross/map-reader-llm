## Summary (Claude's synthesis — advisory, unverified)

Vassányi and Gede train a Mask Regional Convolutional Neural Network (MRCNN) to vectorise point symbols on scanned sheets of the Third Military Survey of Austria-Hungary, and in substrate and task shape it is the closest prior art we have in the historical-map lineage: point symbols rather than areas or lines, on late-nineteenth-century 1 : 200 000 topographic sheets that depict, in the paper's words, "territories of present Romania and Bulgaria, near the Danube river". The whole-sheet test is the "43°44° Svistov" sheet — Svishtov sits on the Bulgarian Danube, which is our geography; the paper itself locates its sheets only as above. A novelty claim about point-symbol extraction from archive maps of this region must clear this paper; what it does not occupy is the archaeological target, the open vocabulary, and the evaluation protocol.

The pipeline is recognisable. Transfer learning from MS COCO weights onto a ResNet101 backbone with a Feature Pyramid Network, all layers trained for four epochs at 1500 steps per epoch, minimum detection confidence 80 per cent. Because the detector cannot swallow a whole sheet, the 10 215 × 14 352 pixel scan is tiled at 1024 pixels with 8 per cent overlap, per-tile CSV outputs are merged, and points closer together than a threshold are collapsed as the same object — our tiling and de-duplication problem, solved by distance alone rather than by agreement across repeated observations. A world file then converts pixel coordinates into projection coordinates.

The results earn the paper its place in our argument. The abstract reports "90% accuracy, while 94% of symbols detected for some categories", and "some categories" means one: the catholic church, 293 correct of 326 detections (89.88 per cent, which Table 1 calls precision, not accuracy) and 293 of the 309 present (94.82 per cent). Elevation points reach 97.52 per cent precision but find only 157 of 336 (46.73 per cent). Watermills (99 present) and orthodox churches (2 present) returned nothing at all. Aggregated across the four categories the sheet yields 450 correct detections of 746 symbols present — 60.3 per cent recall, at 92.4 per cent precision over 487 detections — our arithmetic from Table 1, not figures the paper reports. There is no F1, no Matthews correlation coefficient, no stated match tolerance, no repeated run and no interval; the contribution underwent single-blind peer review on submitted abstracts.

Two failure diagnoses transfer directly. Parameters were tuned on single cropped images, where detection was clean, then carried to a complete sheet where accuracy held for one category of four — a transfer tax between a small tuning set and the deployment substrate, which the authors tentatively attribute to a scale mismatch between hand-cut training crops and the sheet's symbols. And an untrained chimney symbol was detected as one of the four trained classes: a closed four-class model with no "none of these" option must assign the most likely trained label, and confidence on such errors sat below that of correct guesses yet still above the 0.8 threshold.
