## Key Finding

Strong interaction: MEDIUM thinking works best at T=0.0 (deterministic),
HIGH thinking works best at T=0.7 (stochastic). Off-diagonal combinations
(MEDIUM+T=0.7, HIGH+T=0.0) show substantially degraded performance.
This interaction was noted in Obs 200 (working notes).
