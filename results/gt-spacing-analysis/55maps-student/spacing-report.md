## Headline interpretation

- **55-map student GT** — pooled median NN: **506.8 m**; p05: **80.1 m**; < 50 m: **1.2%**; < 70 m: **3.4%**; < 100 m: **10.3%**.
- **Gold-standard reference maps (for comparison)** — pooled median NN: **449.5 m**; p05: **83.2 m**; < 50 m: **0.0%**; < 70 m: **0.4%**; < 100 m: **10.0%**.

Interpret match-zone overlap risk by comparing NN distance against twice the buffer radius (i.e. the buffer *diameter*). For a 50 m buffer diameter (25 m radius), truth buffers begin to overlap when NN < 50 m; at NN < 25 m, truth centres lie inside each other's buffers. If the student GT exhibits materially more sub-50 m spacing than the gold-standard corpus, the F1 plateau at ~40-50 m could plausibly reflect a ceiling imposed by matching ambiguity rather than VLM capability.
