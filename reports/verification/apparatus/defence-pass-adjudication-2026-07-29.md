## Meta-finding

The most recurrent root cause across the twelve is **internal
inconsistency within the lodged registration itself** — the same
parameter specified two or three ways, with execution following one of
them: K=10/any-run vs 5-pass/≥3-of-5 (F6); 361 vs ~360 vs a 321
reserve (F7); label-gated clustering vs label-blind majority vote and
evaluation (F8); 64 px overlap vs constant-fraction cost arithmetic
(F11); one-tailed rule vs registered two-tailed instances (F2); a
rationale block read against its own operative scope (F1). This is a
finding about preregistration authoring, not misconduct, and belongs
in the paper's Discussion.
