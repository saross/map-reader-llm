## Conventions used in the tables

- **Tests.** "tile-swap" is the round-robin paired tile-swap permutation (10,000 draws, seed 42, plus BH): the D42 test. "per-sheet" is the 55-sheet paired sign-swap permutation used by the stride and 3.7 campaigns. These per-sheet tests are not yet D42-conformant. Bracketed `[r2 …]` values are my reads of `results/55map-final-board-r2-2026-09-06/final_board_50m.json` → `pairwise` (595 pairs) or `results/55map-leaderboard/55map_leaderboard_50m_r2.json` → `pairwise` (28 pairs), both read 2026-10-06.
- **Executions.** The dates come from the pass register `results/passes-manifest.json` (`timestamps`) and the run and pass metas under `outputs/` (pass metas, read 2026-10-06). The full list is in § "Execution dates used". "SAME" means the same passes and the same verifier leg (nested contrasts: a threshold, rung or sweep point of one run). "ADJACENT" means consecutive runs within one campaign, less than 1 day apart.
- **Nested contrasts** (threshold, first-N rung, or a verifier swap on an identical union) share most of their output. The D46 floor, the 95th percentile of |ΔF1| between unpaired replicate runs, is therefore not the right comparator for them. The relevant floor is the replicate spread of the *contrast*, for example k3 − k4 measured on disjoint pass subsets, and it is not yet measured.
- **Floors used in the per-section screens** (`planning/w27-configuration-level-testing-2026-10-05.md` § 2): Era-1 340 tiles 0.034; GS 487 tiles 0.030; H10 327 tiles 0.029; 55-map 0.009 at 20 m and 0.012 at 50 m. Cross-execution single-pass pairs on 487 tiles: median 0.037 / 0.061, maximum 0.047 / 0.072. These are single-pass floors. The D46 consensus floors are not measured yet and should be smaller, so the screens are an upper-bound filter.
