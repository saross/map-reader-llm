## 2. Proposed `proposer_pools`

The block is `results/run-conditions.json:12906`, currently `{}`. The order
mirrors the run's `verifier_passes` order, as the stride precedent does:

```json
"proposer_pools": {
 "g512_ov064": {"modality": "text", "path": "g512_ov064"},
 "g512_ov256": {"modality": "text", "path": "g512_ov256"},
 "g384_ov048": {"modality": "text", "path": "g384_ov048"},
 "g384_ov192": {"modality": "text", "path": "g384_ov192"}
}
```

**Why these keys.**

- **The closest precedent is `stride-phaseb-2026-08-25`**
  (`results/run-conditions.json:13428-13447`). It is the same study family:
  four geometry cells, K = 10, `detect_brief-text`, MINIMAL, T = 0.7, flex.
  - Its pools are keyed by the geometry directory, with
    `{"modality": "text", "path": "<dir>"}`.
  - Its verifier keys are `<pool>-union-k10-verify`, which is the grid's own
    verifier-key pattern (`g384_ov192-union-k10-verify`, …).
  - So the keys above make each verifier key's prefix equal to its proposer
    pool, as in stride.
- **Other runs follow the same pattern.** `stride-55map-2026-08-25`,
  `stride-phasec-2026-08-25`, `image-b-gs-2026-08-28`,
  `gemini37-55map-2026-08-29` and `gemini37-screen-2026-08-28` all key pools
  by their geometry directory, with `path` equal to the key.
- **`k-ladder-2026-09-12` is not a registered run.** Its tier-E legs sit
  under this run's `verifier_passes`.
- **Modality is `text`.** In every meta, `configuration.include_example_images`
  is `false` and `instruction_file` is `detect_brief-text.md`. The run's
  verifier hints and the stride pools say `text` too.
- **Leave the conditions alone.** The 15 conditions name
  `proposer_pool: "brief-text"` with `source_run: "grid-2026-08-18"`. Adding
  the geometry keys changes nothing for them:
  - The `pool-unresolved` WARN stays suppressed by `source_run`
    (`scripts/verify_run_conditions.py:342`).
  - `_pool_union_coverage` (`generate_post_run_report.py:1232`) still returns
    `None` for `brief-text`.
  - The k1 and uplift worklists (`scripts/lib_uplift_supplement.py:984`,
    `:1019-1066`) and `build_k_ladder_inventory.py:137` key on the condition's
    pool, so they are unaffected.
- **Re-pointing the conditions to the geometry keys is a separate PI call.**
  Stride's conditions do use geometry keys. Doing the same here would change
  15 conditions-manifest rows, switch on the n_passes and coverage checks, and
  orphan the `("grid-2026-08-18", "brief-text")` family name at
  `build_k_ladder_inventory.py:137`.

**Run `_note` follow-up.** The note at `results/run-conditions.json:13302`
says the pools were "never decomposed into proposer_pools", which becomes

- The recovery's single tile is exactly the primary's one `failed_items`
  entry, a JSON parse error. These are the six D12 tiles in
  `reports/defect-register-2026-08-18.md:42`.

  entries).
- So the union equals `n_dispatched`.

Without the fold, all six rows would read `partial` (for example 486/487).

  g384_ov048 run_3) equals the per-item sum.
